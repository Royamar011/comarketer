# Blog type: How-to guide

Example titles: "How to Run Payroll for a Small Team: A 2026 Guide", "Complete Guide to Email Deliverability (2026)"

## Suggest this type when
The keyword starts with how to, guide, what is, or describes a process or problem ("run payroll", "reduce customer churn").

## Confirm before research
- Depth: standard guide or pillar guide (suggest from the length of top results)
- Include a section on how the company's product helps: default yes for own blog

## Research must fetch
How the process works today, where it breaks, real examples, data and studies, common mistakes, questions people ask (forums, reviews, support content), and the company pages that relate to each step.

## Default length
2,000 to 3,000 words. Pillar: 4,000 plus.

## Outline template

```
[Intro: intent-driven]
H2: What Is [Topic]?
H2: How [Topic] Works
H2: Does Your [Business] Need [Topic]? / Signs It Is Time
H2: How to [Do the Thing]: Step by Step
  H3: Step 1: [Action-led step] ... (5 to 8 steps)
H2: Common [Topic] Mistakes and How to Avoid Them
H2: How [Company] Makes [Topic] Easier   (own blog only)
H2: Frequently Asked Questions
```

## Rules
- Steps are actions the reader can take today, each with what good looks like.
- Every step that involves a decision explains the trade-off.
