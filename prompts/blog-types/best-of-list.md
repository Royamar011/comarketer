# Blog type: Best-of list

Example titles: "9 Best Email Marketing Apps for Shopify (2026)", "7 Best Project Management Tools for Agencies"

## Suggest this type when
The keyword contains best, top, tools, software, apps, platforms, solutions, or a plural category ("project management apps").

## Confirm before research (one screen, prefilled)
- Number of items: suggest from the top results (most common count, rounded to 5, 7, 9 or 10). Longer lists suit competitive keywords.
- Tools to include: proposed from the competitor scan. User can untick or add. Never include brand-listed "never mention" competitors.
- Company position: from the brief's blog defaults (default for own blog: first).
- Comparison table on (default yes).

## Research must fetch, for every tool on the list
Real features, pricing with plan names, who it is for, integrations, ratings with source, from the tool's own site or app listing. Never from memory. Store each page.

## Default length
2,500 to 3,500 words. Pillar keywords: 4,000 to 5,000.

## Outline template (option A follows this; B and C change 2 to 3 sections but keep criteria, the list and FAQ)

```
[Intro: intent-driven, the real situation]
H2: What Is [Topic]?
H2: Why [Audience] Need [Solution] / Signs You Need [Solution]
H2: What to Look for in [the Best] [Topic]
  H3: [Specific criterion 1] ... (4 to 6 criteria)
H2: [X] Best [Topic] for [Audience] ([Year])
  [Comparison table: Tool | Best for | Starting price]
  H3: 1. [Tool]: [short positioning line]
  H3: 2 to X. [Other tools]
H2: How to Choose the Right [Topic] for Your [Business]
H2: Frequently Asked Questions
```

## Entry template for each tool

```
H3: [Number]. [Tool name]: [short positioning line]
[Image brief: product screenshot or dashboard]
[2 to 3 paragraphs: who uses it, what problem it solves, why it is on this list, what makes it distinct]
Best suited for: [one specific sentence]
Key features: [4 to 5 bullets, each explained]
Pros: [3 bullets]
Cons: [2 bullets]
Pricing: [plans and prices from research, or "Not published" with source]
```

## The company's own entry
- 2 to 3 H4s covering capability areas. Functional, descriptive H4s only.
- 2 to 3 paragraphs per H4
- Ratings from the brief (no review counts), 1 to 2 customer quotes from the brief, pricing anchor
- End with a soft CTA to the brief's main CTA link or the most relevant product page

## Positioning other tools
Honest pros and cons. Enterprise-heavy tools: better for large organisations. Lighter tools: good for simpler needs, limited for growth. Never criticise without a sourced reason.
