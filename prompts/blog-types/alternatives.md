# Blog type: Alternatives

Example titles: "7 Best Mailchimp Alternatives for Growing Teams (2026)", "Alternatives to Spreadsheets for Invoicing"

## Suggest this type when
The keyword contains alternative, alternatives, replace, instead of, or "competitors".

## Confirm before research
- The product or method being replaced
- Number of alternatives and which to include (proposed from research)
- Company position (default for own blog: first)

## Research must fetch
Why people leave the original (reviews, forums, complaints with sources), and for each alternative the same fields as a best-of list.

## Default length
2,000 to 3,000 words.

## Outline template

```
[Intro: why people look for alternatives, in their words]
H2: Why [Teams] Look for [Original] Alternatives  [sourced reasons]
H2: What to Look for in a [Original] Alternative  [criteria tied to those reasons]
H2: [X] Best [Original] Alternatives ([Year])   [comparison table]
  H3: 1. [Alternative]: [short positioning line] ... (entry template from best-of list)
H2: How to Switch from [Original] Without Losing [Data or Customers]
H2: Frequently Asked Questions
```

## Rules
- Describe the original fairly. Reasons to leave must come from sourced complaints, not assumption.
