# Blog type: Tips

Example titles: "9 Ways to Reduce SaaS Churn (2026)", "7 Tips to Improve Email Open Rates"

## Suggest this type when
The keyword contains ways to, tips, ideas, strategies, increase, reduce, improve, or boost.

## Confirm before research
- Number of tips (suggest from top results)

## Research must fetch
Proven tactics with examples and data, what top results already list (so ours go further), and where the company's product supports each tip.

## Default length
1,800 to 2,800 words.

## Outline template

```
[Intro: the goal and why it is hard today]
H2: Why [Goal] Matters for [Audience]  [with sourced data]
H2: [X] Ways to [Achieve Goal]
  H3: 1. [Action-led tip] ... each: what to do, why it works, an example, how to measure it
H2: How to Measure [Goal]
H2: Frequently Asked Questions
```

## Rules
- Every tip is specific and actionable. No generic advice.
- Mention the company's product only where it genuinely supports a tip.
